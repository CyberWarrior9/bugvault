## Proposal assertions deliberately corrected

- "Missing A" is not a confirmed dangle: AAAA/other types may exist, and errors or
  uncollected types are unknown. A provider error page is not namespace availability.
- NS mismatches and wildcards do not establish claimability. NS impact is conditional
  on actual delegated control and is not always the highest severity or whole-zone
  control. A wildcard alone does not generate a takeover opportunity.
- Record type describes a possible dependency, not a fixed impact ceiling. MX/SPF/
  DKIM references alone do not grant mail interception or authenticated brand mail.
- Parent CSP/cookie/CORS/OAuth references support conditional priority, **not
  already-proven impact**. CSP allowlisting supplies no injection point; absent CSP
  does not imply injection. A URL parameter is not an OAuth allowlist.
- Domain-cookie matching uses label boundaries and explicit host-only semantics;
  leading dots are not wildcard powers. No two-label public-suffix/site guess is
  authoritative. HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite and cookie prefixes remain relevant.
- HTTPS 200 and certificate-like response headers cannot validate a certificate.
  Browser/Burp interception is not evidence of the origin's public certificate.
- A before-claim response alone is not the distinct negative control, and a marker
  at the provider URL is not control of the candidate hostname.
- No new `dnsrecords`/`dnsreferral` live collectors, auto-registration, provider
  freshness promise or model-router/provider-broadcast pipeline is supplied.
- Candidate states never equal finding `confirmed`; unit/lab/judgment tests are
  bounded gates, not a real-provider rehearsal, live chain or complete coverage.
