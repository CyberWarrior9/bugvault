### Takeaway

Route eight pathways by their actual sink and hand off across existing ledgers; preserve blocked/inconclusive branches rather than manufacturing an RCE verdict.

## What safety, evidence, artifact and acceptance gates should govern an RCE claim?

### Takeaway

Require an attributable **server-side, operator-owned, ephemeral effect**, reproduced with a fresh marker and defeated by a matched negative control, before the validator calls it execution. Keep discovery, evaluation, callbacks, content delivery and timing in weaker states; severity comes from the exact demonstrated trust boundary, not from an arbitrary data harvest.

### Cited Findings

- Current findings enforcement checks existence/distinct hashes of replay/control and impact files; it does **not** independently judge whether they show server execution. The validator requires scope, component/version/config/role prerequisites, deterministic replay, a control that should fail, distinct privilege gain, root-cause dedup and minimal demonstrated impact; `reporter.md` forbids reporting unperformed steps and demands a negative control. — [recon/findings.py:1–16, 209–246](../../recon/findings.py); [recon/prompts/validator.md:18–58](../../recon/prompts/validator.md); [recon/prompts/reporter.md:5–26](../../recon/prompts/reporter.md)
- Upload's `executed` check already requires distinct effect/replay/control artifacts and an attributable consumer log, and SSRF's correlator returns `executed: False`; neither supplies a universal RCE semantic oracle. `factcheck` supports raw captured byte presence only and treats incomplete/encoded bodies conservatively. — [recon/upload.py:826–905](../../recon/upload.py); [recon/ssrf.py:368–382](../../recon/ssrf.py); [recon/factcheck.py:183–221](../../recon/factcheck.py)
- Program authorization is broader than a URL check: `scope-policy` names exact path, method, mode, rate, total budget, burst and resource ownership; the dedicated browser/Burp path needs connected tools and verified routing. Burp MCP is **not** connected to native limits or response IDs, and its active tools require approval. Stop on CAPTCHA/challenge, 429, unexpected side effects or exhausted caps; upload has explicit host stops and cleanup receipts. — [.claude/skills/scope-policy/SKILL.md:8–34](../../.claude/skills/scope-policy/SKILL.md); [.claude/skills/scope-policy/references/burp-workflow.md:29–35, 47–74](../../.claude/skills/scope-policy/references/burp-workflow.md); [recon/upload.py:129–138, 1040–1090, 1190–1225](../../recon/upload.py); [AGENTS.md:118–158](../../AGENTS.md)

The existing test suite uses offline/owned-loopback fixtures, not target authorization or live-target certification. — [AGENTS.md:106–115, 145–158](../../AGENTS.md); [recon/README.md:648–664, 699–716, 729–730, 819–842](../../recon/README.md); [Handover.md:438–451, 599–608](../../Handover.md)

### Inferences

**Proof contract, fail closed:** (1) a source/reference or serialized marker is a `lead`; (2) exact reached sink, arithmetic evaluation, callback, worker processing or repeatable timing is `behavior-observed`, with its own narrow label; (3) `server-execution-verified` needs an operator-approved harmless one-shot primitive that produces an **independently verifiable server-generated result** absent from the initiating request/control (for example, an owned ephemeral job-log event or bounded nonce transformation attributable to a test worker), plus complete request/response/worker provenance, replay with a *new* nonce and a distinct negative control. A raw echoed marker already supplied in the request, an error, a static source hit, a DNS interaction, a `200`, or a timing delta never suffices. (4) `impact-proven` additionally needs an allowed, narrowly owned consequential action/readback and the validator's reachability/precondition/downstream/reportability decision; no automatic severity by class. — [recon/prompts/validator.md:9–36, 38–105](../../recon/prompts/validator.md); [recon/factcheck.py:183–221](../../recon/factcheck.py); [recon/upload.py:826–905](../../recon/upload.py); [recon/ssrf.py:368–382](../../recon/ssrf.py)

**Artifact bundle and privacy:** use immutable campaign-local private artifacts with SHA-256, capture basis and completeness, original import/response/request IDs (distinguishing native from Burp), exact operation/input/sink/worker and account/role/tenant/workflow, policy snapshot, timestamps, test account/object ownership and server/log attribution. Label `baseline`, `trigger`, `fresh replay`, `negative control`, optional `owner worker log`, `bounded impact` and `cleanup`; include the distinct control nonce and evidence that the derived output was absent from request/control. Check hashes and source/context staleness on read/export. Redact cookies, tokens, environment values, private file contents, collaborator metadata and source excerpts before model-facing/exported views; do not pretend hash equality certifies evidence meaning. No body-only verifier should declare non-text, compressed, truncated or oversized data positive without a bounded, provenance-tracked decoder and a non-reflection control. — [recon/store.py:70–97, 479–525](../../recon/store.py); [recon/factcheck.py:183–221](../../recon/factcheck.py); [recon/findings.py:1–16](../../recon/findings.py); [recon/README.md:648–664, 729–730](../../recon/README.md); [AGENTS.md:106–115](../../AGENTS.md)

**Production safety and stops:** require explicit written permission for *execution proof*, not merely a general POST, upload or `execution` ledger label; document exact host/port/path, input, command/function category, owned disposable object, callback destination/second-hop address, methods, role, traffic/time/CPU/object/byte caps, operator observer and cleanup owner before any request. No reverse/web shells, persistence or dropped code, active executable uploads, dangerous serializer gadgets, memory-corruption/DoS, destructive DB/filesystem changes, resource exhaustion, third-party/user data, credential/environment dumps, external exfiltration or CI deployment mutation. Avoid long sleeps and blind payloads whose side effects are unknown.
